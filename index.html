01

Smart Street Light for Energy Conservation

AVR · Arduino IDE · C++ · Sensors — April 2025

An automated street-lighting system built around an AVR microcontroller that watches for real-time vehicle movement. Threshold-based logic and relay switching turn the lights on only when a car actually passes, cutting the over-lighting that comes from lights left on all night. The hardware prototype pairs a motion sensor with a relay module and DC supply.

02

Aquaponic System — Soilless Plant Cultivation

Sensors · Relay logic — February 2025

A small aquaponic platform for growing plants without soil, using fish to close the nutrient loop. A soil-moisture sensor and relay module drive a DC water pump automatically, circulating water between the fish tank and the plants and cutting the manual watering effort out of the system entirely.
